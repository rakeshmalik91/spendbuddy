Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the ExpenseIQ app (package: com.rakeshmalik.expenseiq), developed by Rakesh Malik. This policy describes what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

a) Account Information

  • When you create an account or sign in, we collect your email address via Firebase Authentication. This is used solely to identify your account and sync your data.

b) SMS Messages (READ_SMS Permission)

  • The app requests permission to read your SMS messages to automatically detect and extract financial transactions (e.g., bank debits, credits, UPI payments).
  • SMS data is processed entirely on your device. No SMS content is sent to any external server.
  • SMS analysis can be turned off at any time from the Sync settings within the app.

c) Email / IMAP Access

  • You may optionally link your Gmail account via a Google App Password to allow the app to scan your emails for transaction notifications.
  • Email content is fetched directly from Gmail's IMAP servers and processed on-device using ML Kit. No email content is stored on or transmitted to our servers.
  • Your IMAP credentials (email and app password) are stored in Firebase Storage as plain-text JSON under your account folder to enable cross-device credential restoration.
  • Email analysis can be turned off at any time from the Sync settings, or you can fully unlink your Gmail account to remove all stored credentials.

d) Financial Transaction Data

  • Transaction records (date, amount, category, notes) that you enter manually or that are extracted from SMS/email are stored in Firebase Storage under your account to enable cloud sync and backup.
  • You may optionally enable End-to-End Encryption to encrypt your transaction data with a password-derived key before it leaves your device. When enabled, the encrypted data is unreadable without your password.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Expense tracking — To display, categorize, and analyze your financial transactions.
  • Cloud sync & backup — To store your data in Firebase Storage so you can access it across devices.
  • Transaction extraction — To parse SMS messages and emails on-device for automatic transaction logging.
We do not sell, share, or monetize your personal data, SMS content, or email content with any third party.

3. Your Choices

You have full control over what data the app accesses:

  • SMS Sync — Can be disabled at any time from the Sync settings. When disabled, the app will not read or process any SMS messages.
  • Email Sync — Can be disabled at any time from the Sync settings. When disabled, the app will not connect to Gmail or process any emails.
  • Unlink Gmail — You can fully unlink your Gmail account, which stops all email scanning and deletes your stored IMAP credentials from Firebase.
  • End-to-End Encryption — Can be enabled or disabled from the Account settings.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication — For user sign-in and account management. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Storage — For cloud storage of your transaction data and IMAP credentials.
  • Google ML Kit — For on-device text extraction from emails. No data is sent to Google's servers.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Transaction data is stored in Firebase Storage under your authenticated account.
  • Optional End-to-End Encryption encrypts your transaction files (CSV) before upload using a key derived from your password.
  • IMAP credentials are stored as plain-text JSON in Firebase Storage. They are not covered by the End-to-End Encryption feature.
  • SMS and email content is processed and discarded on-device; it is never uploaded to Firebase or any other server.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your data is retained in Firebase Storage as long as your account exists.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app's Account settings. Upon deletion, all cloud-stored data is permanently removed.
  • You can unlink your Gmail account at any time, which deletes the stored IMAP credentials from Firebase.

7. Children's Privacy

ExpenseIQ is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
